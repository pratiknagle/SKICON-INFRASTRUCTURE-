Sponsor Profile

The sponsor and its track record

THE DEVELOPER

Skicon Infrastructure Co

Manek Ventures is the business brand associated with the Manek family of Bhandup West. Real estate activity operates under Skicon Infrastructure Co, identified in the diligence profile as a partnership firm.

The family business history dates to 1982, with Skicon Infrastructure reported to have been established in 2006. The group also has interests in local print media and hospitality.
